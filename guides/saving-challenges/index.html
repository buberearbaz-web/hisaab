Saving challenge

3 money saving challenges that actually stick

A challenge turns saving into a game with a finish line. Pick one, give the money a home before you start, and tick it off as you go.

2 min read · Updated 24 Oct 2026

1. The small-spends check

Chai, delivery fees and quick top-ups feel like nothing. Over a year they add up:

A dayA monthA year
₹50₹1,500₹18,250
₹100₹3,000₹36,500
₹200₹6,000₹73,000
₹500₹15,000₹1,82,500

The challenge: log every small spend for one week. Cut the ones you won't miss and move that amount to a saving goal each week. Look for money leaks while you're at it: forgotten subscriptions, delivery and small-order fees, late fees and bank charges.

2. The 52-week challenge

Save ₹100 in week 1, ₹200 in week 2, and keep adding ₹100 a week up to ₹5,200 in week 52. Tick a box every week and you finish the year with:

₹100 + ₹200 + … + ₹5,200₹1,37,800

Week 52 feels hard? Do it in reverse: start with ₹5,200 while you're keen and end on ₹100. The printable tracker is at the bottom of this page.

3. The no-spend month

For one month, tick every day you spend nothing beyond the essentials.

Still allowed

Rent and bills, groceries from a list, medicine, travel to work.

Not this month

Takeaway and delivery, online sales, new clothes, impulse buys.

For example, skipping QAR 50 a day for 31 days keeps QAR 1,550. Move it to a saving goal at the end of the month, before it drifts back into spending.

Before any unplanned buy

Ask four questions. Is it in this month's budget? Would you buy it at full price? Will you use it 10 times or more? Do you still want it after 48 hours? Four yeses means buy it, guilt-free.

Make it stick

  • Give the money a home the same day, in a separate saving goal.
  • Do it with someone. A friend or your family keeps you going.
  • Missed a week? Carry on. One gap doesn't undo the rest.

Example amounts, not financial advice.
